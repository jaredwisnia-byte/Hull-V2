# Keep Hull productive through controlled improvement

**Hull should adopt a small factory operating discipline: clear station contracts, traceable inventory, independent qualification, and proportionate maintenance.** This should simplify the project by reducing repeated diagnosis, uncertain handoffs and accidental promotion. Hull already has foreman-like repair controls, stage review, QA, CI, source inventory, regression machinery and versioned experience records; adding another hierarchy would duplicate useful work. The priority is connecting those mechanisms and closing specific gaps, especially candidate/release separation, configuration-triggered checks and independent measurements. Factory roles describe responsibilities, not mandatory bots, classes or services. Experiments—including failures and uncertain proposals—must remain available for learning, while approval of production outputs stays controlled. A new ERP/WMS, permanent runtime LLM management team or broad rewrite would overcomplicate the project.

Research reference date: **November 11, 2026, America/Toronto**. Public-source findings and a read-only Hull source audit inform this report. Recommendations below are proposed mappings, not changes implemented by this study; no new builds, maintenance jobs or release controls were executed.

## Factory practice supports fewer, stronger controls

Toyota's useful lesson is **make abnormalities visible and stop their propagation at the affected process**. Its public Production System explanation connects jidoka with Just-in-Time, aims to eliminate continuous human machine-watching, and says work and abnormality criteria should be understood before automation. Standard work, andon signals and direct investigation provide a practical operating loop. Toyota's glossary also treats over-processing and unnecessarily complex tools as waste—an appropriate test for Hull's proposed management layer. ([Toyota TPS](https://global.toyota/en/company/vision-and-philosophy/production-system/), [Toyota glossary](https://mag.toyota.co.uk/toyota-production-system-glossary/))

GE's historical Six Sigma experience and today's GE Aerospace Lean system need separate labels. A GE participant's 2002 account describes the initial 1995 manufacturing focus and later attention to customer issues and delivery variation. Its publicly accessible abstract supports that history, not private operating instructions. GE Aerospace's FLIGHT DECK, launched in 2024, publicly emphasizes standard work, visual management, flow/pull and problem-solving. Its June 2026 Greenville account describes observing work directly and investigating with operators; this is company-reported practice, not independent proof of productivity effects. ([GE historical account](https://asq.org/quality-resources/articles/an-inside-look-at-six-sigma-at-ge?id=83c5624f5fbf4688bd3d1af434081c9a), [FLIGHT DECK overview](https://www.geaerospace.com/news/articles/momentum-matters-year-life-flight-deck), [Greenville case](https://www.geaerospace.com/news/articles/flight-deck-not-so-secret-sauce-strengthening-team-safety-and-performance-greenville))

Practical Six Sigma means defining a defect, validating its measurement, investigating variation, testing a countermeasure and sustaining the improvement. ASQ's DMAIC supplies that sequence without requiring a belt hierarchy. For Hull, define critical quality conditions before optimizing: required shape/features, geometry integrity, supported export, uncertainty and resource limits. Triangle count is a constraint, not a universal quality score. ([ASQ DMAIC](https://asq.org/quality-resources/dmaic))

Ford's accessible **July 2023** supplier requirements provide concrete historical mechanisms: reaction steps in work instructions, measurement-system analysis, containment, root-cause correction and reverse FMEA—deliberately challenging equipment/error-proofing to see whether defects escape. Findings feed back into control plans. The official index lists newer Ford documents effective November 9, 2026, but their PDFs were not successfully read during this research. These examples therefore establish useful historical practices, not verified current Ford compliance requirements. ([Ford 2023 requirements](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Ford_IATF_CSR.pdf), [current IATF index](https://www.iatfglobaloversight.org/oem-requirements/customer-specific-requirements/))

Mitsubishi requires specificity. **Mitsubishi Electric** publishes preventive-maintenance activities including inspections, interlock checks, communications verification and findings reports; its e-F@ctory support module offers cycle-time monitoring, guard bands and timestamped 5M change records. **Mitsubishi Motors**, a separate company, describes an internal audit division independent of business execution. Transfer equipment health checks, change histories and audit separation; do not copy corporate structures or infer universal maintenance intervals. ([Electric maintenance](https://us.mitsubishielectric.com/fa/en/service/cnc-service-solutions/cnc-preventative-maintenance/), [Electric monitoring](https://us.mitsubishielectric.com/fa/en/products/software/visualization/support-module/), [Motors audit governance](https://www.mitsubishi-motors.com/en/sustainability/governance/policy.html))

Shenzhen is a diverse manufacturing ecosystem, not one operating standard. Seeed's firsthand documentation offers useful examples: engineering/design/production validation answer different readiness questions, pilot results feed improvements, and the appropriate subset of stages depends on the project. Its supplier-authored materials describe early manufacturability feedback, test fixtures and unit tracking. Hull can borrow the short build-test-redesign loop and design-for-testability, while recognizing these are one supplier's documented practices rather than audited ecosystem-wide outcomes. ([Seeed validation guide](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/service_for_fusion_sample_validation/), [Seeed DFX announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/seeed-releases-fusion-design-for-x-service-to-optimize-product-manufacturability-300851037.html), [2026 catalog](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/wiki-platform/2026_Seeed_Studio_Catalog.pdf))

## Hull needs stronger boundaries around existing machinery

The source audit found a substantial base. `correction_review` checks declared improvement and bounded safeguards; `stage_review` interprets stage evidence; `qa` measures exported geometry; `pipeline` supplies orchestration callbacks and freshness checks. These are worth retaining. Their existence does not prove every quality gauge is independently correct: the correction reviewer receives caller-supplied measurements, while stage review shares pipeline evidence. A static inventory maps code and configuration but cannot certify execution or test coverage. ([correction_review.py](../../long-lines/tools/propkit/correction_review.py#L16), [stage_review.py](../../long-lines/tools/propkit/stage_review.py#L11), [qa.py](../../long-lines/tools/propkit/qa.py#L82), [audit_inventory.py](../../long-lines/tools/audit_inventory.py#L20))

Three findings deserve attention before another management feature:

| Verified source finding | Proposed response |
|---|---|
| `factory.py` updates the library manifest before `qa.check`; later `needs_person` metadata does not prove candidate/release segregation. | Build under candidate IDs; keep approved outputs available; promote the approved artifact/manifest through an explicit gate. Consumer contamination is a risk inference, not a proven finding about every consumer. |
| `hull-contracts.yml` path filters omit requirements and several factory configuration inputs. | Add dependency/configuration trigger coverage and a capability-tagged test manifest. A configuration-only change can alter output. |
| Hosted Python 3.11 installs unpinned scientific libraries; the documented local worker uses a different stack. Optional vision/CAD requirements are already separately pinned. | Declare a tested compatibility/install matrix; record runtime identity and show required capabilities separately from unavailable optional ones. |

These findings come from [factory.py](../../long-lines/tools/factory.py#L535), [hull-contracts.yml](../../.github/workflows/hull-contracts.yml#L4), [vision requirements](../../requirements-vision.txt) and [CAD requirements](../../requirements-cad.txt). Missing CAD capability can legitimately skip CAD tests for a mesh-only change; it cannot qualify a CAD release.

Warehouse discipline means tracing material identity and disposition. GS1's framework connects important tracking events with their identifying/context data. Hull's analogue is one existing manifest/event stream linking inputs, model checkpoints, dependencies, configuration, intermediate artifacts, attempts and exports through a run ID. Use explicit states—unverified, experimental, work in progress, quarantined, approved, retired—and preserve parentage across repairs. Hashes establish identity, not fitness. A proposed receiving check verifies format, view assumptions, units, provenance and supported runtime before expensive work. Cache validity follows relevant input/code/configuration/runtime identities. This does not require a new warehouse database service. ([GS1 traceability](https://support.gs1.org/support/solutions/articles/43000734535-what-is-the-gs1-traceability-standard-))

The compact responsibility model is:

| Responsibility | Hull implementation principle |
|---|---|
| Station/operator | Transform inputs deterministically where possible; emit evidence against a versioned contract. |
| Foreman/supervisor | Try bounded repairs and local retries; contain affected work without silently weakening requirements. |
| Auditor | Independently check selected outputs, provenance, required tests and promotion criteria; do not repair the candidate being judged. |
| Maintenance | Verify toolchain, caches, capacity, recovery and test executability; qualify upgrades before promotion. |
| Improvement owner | Investigate recurring loss, compare hypotheses and update standards using demonstrated results. |

These can be functions or commands sharing the current infrastructure. One person can cover several duties; independence comes from separate oracles, protected evaluation data and reviewed configuration authority, not merely a different job title.

## Independent measurement must protect both learning and release

**Statistical stability, conformance and physical truth are different questions.** NIST's capability guidance assumes an adequately characterized stable process; its measurement guidance uses check standards to detect bias and variability changes. Repeatedly inspecting one stylized leg with correlated, uncalibrated probes establishes neither generalization nor Six Sigma capability. A stable wrong gauge still produces wrong answers. Start with stratified run comparisons and measurement-system checks, not Cpk, DPMO or capability badges. ([NIST capability](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section1/pmc16.htm), [NIST measurement control](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section2/mpc22.htm))

Hull already has `diag/regress_assets.py` and `geometry_baseline.json`, comparing car/coffee/sofa/wallphone geometry measures. Reuse that runner rather than inventing another framework. Source inspection shows it temporarily modifies shared library/ledger/report records and restores them in `finally`; future qualification should use an isolated workspace. Its opt-in `--update-baseline` can save failed results. Saving failures is legitimate characterization; the missing distinction is controlled labeling and promotion authority, not evidence of autonomous self-certification. ([regression runner](../../long-lines/tools/diag/regress_assets.py#L1), [existing baseline](../../long-lines/factory/geometry_baseline.json))

Keep three meanings explicit. An **observed baseline** records what happened, including known failures and uncertainty. A **golden regression reference** is an explicitly reviewed, versioned comparison authority and can document acknowledged limitations. **Release approval** means the candidate met the applicable release criteria. Retain all experiment/observation records and their provenance; manage bulky artifacts through a declared retention policy that preserves important reproducers. Failed ideas must remain searchable instead of disappearing behind rejection. Changing an observation does not authorize changing the golden reference or approving a release.

The proposed independent qualification set has three classes: analytic/synthetic objects with known geometry and camera parameters; real calibrated multiview/reference assets when available; and negative/adversarial controls such as painted-only recesses, inconsistent views, wrong units, stale artifacts, missing inputs and corrupt caches. Declare preset assistance separately from automatic intake. Reused development fixtures remain valuable regressions but cease to be untouched holdouts. Qualification must report each required class and capability rather than hide a broken class in an average.

Audit the auditor: deliberately introduce a unit mismatch, lost hole, invalid ownership or disabled gate and verify detection and containment. Separate fitting objectives from evaluation measurements where possible. A software audit can establish reproducibility, numerical behavior and policy compliance; it cannot discover invisible physical geometry from an ambiguous image. That uncertainty should inform the output's disposition rather than suppress exploration.

## Maintain software through scoped checks and readable contracts

Google's public engineering guidance favors small self-contained changes, code-health improvement and separating substantial refactoring from behavior changes. Its SRE practices connect incidents with preventive actions, measure operational toil and compare bounded candidate deployments with a control. Apple publicly recommends purpose/dependency-based test plans and representative performance measurements; instrumentation can alter timing. These are transferable mechanisms. They do not establish Apple's secret internal culture or justify adopting Swift/Xcode for Hull. ([Google small changes](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/developer/small-cls.html), [Google review standard](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/reviewer/standard.html), [SRE postmortems](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/), [Apple test plans](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/organizing-tests-to-improve-feedback), [Apple performance tests](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/writing-and-running-performance-tests))

Hull's proposed maintenance cadence separates cheap routine controls from qualification. Code does not wear like a spindle; environment drift, corrupted data, incompatible dependencies and accumulated complexity are the relevant failure modes.

| Trigger | Proposed minimum work |
|---|---|
| Session startup or changed runtime fingerprint | Check checkout/configuration, required imports and tool/model identities, output readability/writability and capacity. Cache healthy identities. |
| Per run | Validate input/frame/unit/schema assumptions, required capabilities, finite values, lineage and freshness; contain the affected candidate on a hard failure. |
| Meaningful code/configuration change | Syntax and relevant contracts/unit tests; boundary integration checks and a regression for changed behavior. Geometry/export edits invoke the applicable Blender/CAD fixtures. |
| Release, toolchain or model upgrade | Isolated candidate environment; full qualification for supported/changed capabilities, independent benchmark classes, representative performance comparison and rollback verification. |
| Initially weekly during rapid development | Brief triage of recurring defects, rework, flaky/skipped checks, slow stations, storage growth and stale documentation; adjust cadence from value. |
| Initially monthly recovery rehearsal, adjusted by risk | Restore a known-good environment and important records; verify clean install, manifest consistency and representative output. Combine with upgrade qualification when appropriate. |

These are starting policies, **not installed scheduled jobs or manufacturer-prescribed intervals**. Do not rebuild every asset for every edit, automatically upgrade production dependencies, or let maintenance rewrite its own acceptance criteria. Compare performance on matching workload classes and cold/warm conditions. Google's toil guidance supports measuring whether automation saves more effort than it costs; its staffing targets are not Hull targets. ([Google eliminating toil](https://sre.google/workbook/eliminating-toil/))

A station contract should state input/output schema, units and coordinate frame, ownership, invariants, approximation budgets, dependencies, evidence, retry limits and escalation. Keep one authoritative configuration model and short operator runbooks. Refactor as real changes expose seams: pure numerical transforms, Blender adapters, orchestration, storage and reporting. Extend existing `pipeline.py` rather than create a parallel production line. Keep current instructions and limitations concise; link historical incidents/experiments and generated source inventory separately. Module size alone is no reason for a rewrite. ([existing pipeline](../../long-lines/tools/propkit/pipeline.py#L41))

## Repair and innovation need different approval lanes

The proposed repair protocol is **contain and preserve evidence → isolate/reproduce → diagnose root and escape causes → test a bounded fix or alternatives → independently verify → restart/promote → add a counterexample and update the runbook**. Identify both why the defect occurred and why controls missed it. Preserve exact input, runtime, intermediate evidence and rollback point. A temporary workaround needs an expiry/removal condition. Verification includes the failing case, neighboring cases and independent checks proportional to risk; a prettier render or plausible five-whys story is insufficient.

Innovation uses the same evidence system with looser entry requirements. A hypothesis can be speculative and a candidate can fail; neither needs production approval to be worth retaining. Record expected effect, controlled variables, alternatives, observations and the next decision. Qualification stays stricter: frozen criteria, declared assistance, protected evaluation and reviewed promotion. Existing versioned SQLite/patch memory supports retrieval and provenance, but it is not trained ML or automatic causal learning. ([Hull build memory](../../long-lines/tools/propkit/build_memory.py#L61))

The actionable first sequence is to close receiving/release and CI trigger gaps; isolate and extend the existing regression runner with known-truth and deliberately bad fixtures; then write concise contracts at high-risk station boundaries. Introduce one maintenance entry point and the repair/experiment record alongside that work. Only then refactor recurring mixed responsibilities or add monitoring justified by observed losses. A frozen isolated asset batch provides Hull's equivalent of a canary comparison without introducing a web deployment service. ([Google canarying](https://sre.google/workbook/canarying-releases/))

Judge progress through diagnosis/repair time, rework compute and human intervention, escaped/repeat defects, first-pass acceptance under unchanged criteria, required-check completeness and representative runtime/memory. Include false rejection: suppressing useful experiments is also loss. Do not optimize test counts, log volume or triangle counts as substitutes for quality. Every proposed checker needs a specific failure mode, independent evidence, an action and a justified cost; duplicated reviewer scores and full rebuilds on trivial edits fail that test.

## Conclusion

The useful factory analogy is a disciplined flow of evidence and authority. Hull can remain exploratory while making it unambiguous which outputs are observations, candidates and approved products. That distinction lets failures teach without quietly becoming the production standard.

The immediate evidence supports a compact control and maintenance layer, not organizational expansion. Its exact cadence, benchmark size and return remain unmeasured. The source audit did not execute the regression runner, prove every consumer path, audit remote permissions or validate a clean restore. Those are the first implementation's questions to resolve through proportionate checks—not reasons to postpone the concrete boundary fixes or claim universal reconstruction capability.
